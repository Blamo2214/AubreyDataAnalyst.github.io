About Me

Connecting the physical barrel to the financial market

I am a data analyst and independent commodity market researcher focused on crude oil, refined products, futures, and derivatives. I am working toward a career in commodity research, trading analytics, or energy-market intelligence, where technical analysis and a practical understanding of market fundamentals inform decisions.

My interest in markets began well before my formal analytics education. Since 2015, I have researched and traded futures and options in a personal account, including crude oil, interest rates, and equity indices. That experience led me to study calendar spreads, option structures, volatility, and the relationship between market narratives and actual price behavior.

Today, I publish independent petroleum-market reporting through Agitlytics. I follow EIA inventory releases, WTI and Brent futures curves, diesel and gasoline crack spreads, refinery conditions, tanker freight, and geopolitical supply risks. My research combines quantitative comparisons with physical-market context, including shipping constraints through the Strait of Hormuz and disruptions affecting major producing and refining regions.

I hold a Bachelor of Science in Data Analytics from Southern New Hampshire University and am pursuing a Master of Science in Data Analytics. I use Python, SQL, R, Excel, Plotly, Power BI, and other tools to develop datasets, analyze statistical relationships, and communicate findings. My aim is not simply to produce a chart, but to explain what the evidence supports, what it does not, and why that distinction matters to a trader, analyst, or decision-maker.

Research Approach

From market fundamentals to actionable analysis

I combine price data, physical supply information, and an understanding of political and operational constraints. My work emphasizes repeatable methods, clearly stated assumptions, and the distinction between evidence, interpretation, and a trade hypothesis.

Crude Oil & Refined-Product Fundamentals

I examine commercial inventories, refinery utilization, crude and product balances, diesel and gasoline cracks, shipping disruptions, and freight costs. I focus on how bottlenecks in one part of the petroleum system affect delivered prices and margins elsewhere.

Derivatives & Market Structure

My independent trading and research cover futures term structures, calendar spreads, volatility regimes, options payoffs, scenario analysis, and event-driven price behavior. I am interested in translating a market outlook into a measurable risk/reward framework.

Geopolitical & Policy Research

I study how sanctions, conflict, trade policy, and government decisions affect energy flows and market expectations. My reporting distinguishes verified developments from open-source claims and considers both short-term headline reactions and longer-term physical constraints.

Data Engineering & Communication

I collect, clean, join, model, and visualize data using Python, SQL, R, and BI tools, then explain the findings in interactive graphics and written research. I also bring experience with databases, business operations, and communicating analyses to nontechnical audiences.
