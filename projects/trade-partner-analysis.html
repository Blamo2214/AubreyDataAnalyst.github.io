Interactive Trade Visualization

U.S.–China Trade Partner Analysis

An animated comparison of how national trade relationships with China and the United States have changed over time. Select countries by continent, move through individual years, or play the full time series to explore shifts in trade orientation.

About the visualization

Each point represents a country. China is plotted on the horizontal axis and the United States on the vertical axis. Point color indicates which partner accounts for the larger share of the selected trade metric. The dashed diagonal line represents equal values between the two partners.

The visualization is generated from a reusable Python and Plotly engine built to support multiple U.S.–China trade metrics, including import share, export share, and future measures added to the underlying dataset.
